# External assets

## Horse model, skeleton and gait clips

* File: `public/assets/horse.glb`
* Source: Mesh2Motion, `static/animations/horse-animations.glb`
  (https://github.com/Mesh2Motion/mesh2motion-app), retrieved 2026-08-28.
* Licence: **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication). The repository's
  `LICENSE-CC0.MD` states: "All 3d models, blend files, rigs, animations —
  CC0 1.0 Universal".
* Contents used: one skinned mesh (2,146 vertices), a 56-bone anatomical
  quadruped skeleton, and the animation clips `Idle`, `Walk`, `Trot`
  (the file also carries `Run`, `Eating`, `Rear` and others, which this game
  does not play).
* The asset is 1.06 MB and carries a single 44 KB palette texture. No 4K or 8K
  maps are shipped anywhere in this project.

### How it was integrated rather than used as-is

* The shipped material is a glossy clearcoat preview shader. It is replaced
  with a `MeshPhysicalMaterial` tuned for this game's two lighting rigs.
* The palette texture is re-graded at load (`restyleCoat` in
  `src/world/horse.ts`): 62 % desaturation, a shadow lift and a warm bias, so
  the animal belongs to the winter light rather than to its preview render.
* A shader injection (`addCoatDetail`) breaks the coat up in object space with
  a three-octave value noise on roughness and albedo, plus a darker underside.
  This is what makes it read as winter coat rather than plastic.
* Stride length drives clip playback rate, so the hooves never skate; hoof
  contacts are detected from the foot bones' world height rather than assumed
  from the clip.
* Breathing, ear movement and nostril flare are layered on top of the clips.

## Everything else

Every other surface, mesh, sound and texture in the game is generated by this
project at runtime:

* Textures — `src/world/textures.ts` (tileable value-noise fields painted to
  canvases; nothing larger than 512 px).
* Bells, strap, sleigh, harness, handler, tack room, terrain, trees, village —
  authored as geometry in `src/world/`.
* Environment maps — painted equirectangular canvases run through
  `PMREMGenerator` (`src/world/sky.ts`). No downloaded HDRI.
* Audio — modal synthesis and filtered noise rendered with
  `OfflineAudioContext` and the Web Audio graph (`src/audio/`). No recordings,
  sampled loops or third-party sound files.
